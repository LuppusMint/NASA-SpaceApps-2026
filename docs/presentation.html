01 · Problem & Physical Context

Why adding MODIS and VIIRS detections creates artificial fire trends

02 / 07
1. Different Pixel Footprints

MODIS observes at 1 km nadir, whereas VIIRS resolves down to 375 m. A single flaming front can trigger one MODIS pixel or several VIIRS detections, multiplying raw counts without extra fire on the ground.

2. Five Constellation Transitions

Between 2000 and 2026 the fleet shifts through Terra-only, Terra+Aqua (2002), VIIRS S-NPP overlap (2012), NOAA-20 (2018), post-2021 MODIS orbital drift (2021), and NOAA-21 (2023).

3. Non-Fire Contamination

Gas flares, smelters, volcanoes, and nocturnal particle artifacts inside the South Atlantic Magnetic Anomaly (SAMA) skew regional totals unless filtered systematically before modeling.

Core principle: Harmonize exposure on a frozen 200 km spatial split inside the 2012–2020 pre-drift overlap before running any trend or anomaly test.

02 · Data Engineering & Modeling Architecture

End-to-end deterministic pipeline from 85M raw rows to harmonized calendars

03 / 07

1. Quality Control & Cubes

  • Processed 85,002,288 raw NASA FIRMS detections out-of-core in DuckDB + Parquet down to 75,556,207 clean vegetation fire detections.
  • Removed 2,703,746 non-vegetation triggers (type IN (1,2,3): volcanoes, offshore, static sources).
  • Masked 358 persistent stationary 10 km cells (2,872,511 chronic flares/industry rows; 472,710 clean-band hits isolated).
  • Repaired 138 negative FRP rows and 39 zero-brightness anomalies; clipped to Natural Earth 1:10m coastlines.
  • Intersected 76,388,628 rows with SAMA bounds (nominal+ filter already eliminates the low-confidence suspect band).

2. Leakage-Free Calibration (M0–M3)

  • Trained strictly on the pre-drift overlap (2012-02 to 2020-12): 930,100 rows across 7,184 50 km cells.
  • 200 km spatial 5-fold cross-validation blocks eliminate spatial autocorrelation leakage across train/test boundaries.
  • M1 robust physical biweight bridge vs. M2 Hurdle LightGBM (logistic occurrence + Tweedie severity with covariate-shift weights).
  • M3 simplex blend + 1D Kalman state relay + non-negative Minimum Trace (MinT) hierarchical reconciliation across all geographic levels.
  • Verified temporal hold-out check (fit 2016–2020, predict 2012–2015): Terra-only R² = 0.808; Terra+Aqua R² = 0.920.

3. Statistical Calendar & Delivery

  • Reference climatology frozen strictly to 2001–2020 so post-2021 extreme years never leak into baseline medians or percentiles.
  • Trends: Theil–Sen slopes with Hamed–Rao AR(1)-corrected Mann–Kendall and Benjamini–Hochberg FDR control (q < 0.05).
  • Early onset: True trailing 90-day sum across year boundaries (INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY for the first 89 days; complete windows, n ≥ 10).
  • Extremes screening: Annual block-maxima GEV via L-moments/PWM with 500 parametric bootstrap replicates (475/510 series pass sanity filters).
  • Web app: Offline-capable WSGI + DuckDB server, 50/50 tests passing, 1-click UTF-8 submission.csv.
