Fire Relay South America

Data: NASA LANCE FIRMS MODIS C6.1 and VIIRS S-NPP / NOAA-20 / NOAA-21 active-fire detections, harmonized on a 10 km equal-area Albers grid; administrative boundaries from Natural Earth 1:10m Cultural Vectors (CC0).

Runs entirely offline on local DuckDB + Parquet. No external CDNs, tiles, fonts or trackers.

Scientific methodology, statistical harmonization, and empirical validation developed by the project team. Software engineering assisted by AI/SI tooling under human verification. Not for tactical wildfire suppression, emergency dispatch, evacuation routing, or life-and-property decisions; no NASA, LANCE, ESDIS, NOAA, or U.S. Government endorsement is implied.
