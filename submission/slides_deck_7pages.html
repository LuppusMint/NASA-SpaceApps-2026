The problem

Five satellites, five regimes, one distorted relay

02 / 07
Footprint

MODIS observes at 1 km nadir; VIIRS at 375 m. One flaming front can produce one MODIS pixel or several VIIRS pixels.

Relay

Terra-only, Terra+Aqua, the MODIS/VIIRS overlap, and the VIIRS backbone are different sampling systems. Adding sensors creates artificial steps.

Contamination

Nocturnal VIIRS, the SAMA particle environment, static gas flares, industry, and volcanoes can enter the record unless explicitly flagged.

Consequence

A naive multi-sensor sum turns instrument history into a fake trend. The distortion concentrates at 2002, 2012, 2018, 2021, 2023.

Sensor relay and regime boundaries2000 → 2026
The design goal: make the sensor identity stop mattering, so a 2001 anomaly and a 2025 anomaly are finally measured on the same footing.

DATADeterministic QC

Clean the physical record before modeling

  • 85,002,288 raw FIRMS detections → 75,556,207 clean detections.
  • 2,703,746 removed by type IN (1,2,3).
  • 2,872,511 removed as persistent static-cell detections across 358 10 km cells.
  • 472,710 clean candidate detections isolated inside static cells.
  • 138 negative FRP and 39 zero-brightness rows corrected.
  • 76,388,628 rows intersect SAMA; 0 rows are removed by the SAMA suspect rule (the suspect class is empty after the nominal+ filter).
MODELLeakage-free benchmark

Calibrate the relay on a frozen overlap

  • 5 spatial block folds frozen in the committed split manifest.
  • 930,100 overlap rows = 465,050 cell-months x 2 replicas, 2012–2020.
  • 7,184 cells at 50 km; dataset SHA-256 ff927d7b…e0d6.
  • M0 raw → M1 physical bridge → M2 hurdle/Tweedie → M3 simplex + Kalman.
  • Admin-1 is a proportional downscaling of the country total; country = sum of Admin-1; non-negative by construction.
  • Cross-regime guard and five relay step-change diagnostics.
